Line balancing & time study

Production engineering / Academic project

Understanding the assembly sequence, finding the bottleneck, and identifying where work could be distributed more evenly.

  • Time studies
  • Line balancing
  • Bottleneck analysis
Problem, method & takeaway

Business problem

Understand how work moves through a Lego-car assembly line and where station workloads become uneven.

Data & method

Collected time-study data at each station, then established the correct build sequence (the precedence of tasks) across stations.

Analysis

Compared station times to find the bottleneck station that paces the line, and calculated the slack time at every other station.

Engineering takeaway

Station-level measurement makes the constraint visible and gives a factual basis for redistributing work.

Battery production optimization

Process analytics / Academic project

Using a designed experiment to understand which production parameters have the greatest influence on charge retention.

  • Minitab
  • Full factorial DOE
  • ANOVA
Problem, method & takeaway

Business problem

Investigate how temperature, charge time, and material type influence battery charge retention.

Data & method

Collected experimental data across a full factorial design of the three factors in Minitab, with charge retention as the response.

Analysis

Used ANOVA to separate real factor effects from run-to-run variation and identify which parameters most improved charge retention.

Engineering takeaway

Structured experimentation separates meaningful process effects from variation, so decisions rest on evidence rather than trial and error.
